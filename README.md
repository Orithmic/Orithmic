# Orithmic

Orithmic aims to make application development and distribution more sustainable.

Whether you have an idea for a tool to boost your efficiency at work, or would like to allow your employees to build and share tools without the complexity or management overhead, or an application you have always wanted to build and monetize at scale. Orithmic is designed to make the entire process simpler and more accessible.

While other platforms lower the initial barrier to entry, they often do so in a way that introduces larger barriers down the line. This hampers future scalability and innovation. True value lies in innovation, which is often the most challenging part of the process. Innovation requires systems that allow you to drill down when necessary without rebuilding from scratch. Orithmic removes these barriers in a more sustainable way that enables more people to innovate without the cost of increased complexity or introducing latent costs. Costs that hamper innovation and force you to make serious compromises.
